# Direction visuelle — 011

- **Format:** vertical 9:16, cinematic, premium, no recognizable faces.
- **Opening:** rapid fragments—news headlines blurred/unreadable, data streams, crowded screens, passing lights, abstract information overload.
- **Pause:** screens go dark; a still glass of water; a quiet landscape; dust floating in a beam of light.
- **Journey motif:** roads, train windows, moving clouds, horizon—symbolize the urge to reach somewhere.
- **Letting go:** a path dissolves into fog or a wide sea; camera gently lifts into open sky, never a literal fall/jump.
- **Closing:** calm, spacious nature, soft golden light; a door-like opening in mist or sunlight through trees.
- Palette: deep blue, turquoise, white, soft gold; minimal typography and generous negative space.
- No portrait/likeness of speaker; no generated voice or music/audio.
- The “abyss” metaphor must be represented as spacious openness, not a cliff, pit, or dangerous jump.
