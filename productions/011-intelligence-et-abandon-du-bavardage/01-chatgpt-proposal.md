# Editorial proposal — 011

## Title
**Et si tu cessais de vouloir aller quelque part ?**

## Core idea
We feed the mind with information, opinions, comparisons and endless stories. But accumulating information is not the same as seeing clearly. Beneath the constant desire to reach, achieve or become, there may be a possibility of simply inhabiting life as it is.

## Editorial framing
The source's paradoxical image of a “bottomless pit” is reframed as letting go of the need for a fixed destination: not a dangerous leap, but an inner openness. Avoid implying that the audience must suppress ambition or that bliss is guaranteed.
