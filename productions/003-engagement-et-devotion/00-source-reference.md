# Original transcript — Production 003: Total involvement

**Original text as supplied by John:**

If you're seeking fulfillment of life, then it needs involvement. Devotion means just unquestioned, unbridled involvement, not holding back anything. Will you respond to any human being totally without holding back anything unless you have a deep sense of love or devotion towards that person? It's only because of tremendous sense of involvement that you respond absolutely without holding back anything. Where there is no involvement, you will do something socially correct. Some of you don't even do that. But most people do something that they think is a courtesy or their duty to do something just about that. But only when you have a very deep sense of love or devotion towards something, you will do everything that you can do. I'm saying in every moment of your life, if you're not doing everything that you can do, what are you saving yourself for? You want to work from your grave? So this profound involvement is called devotion. Devotion is not a deal. Deal makers are different people. Devotees are different people. Nothing need to happen to me. I will throw myself into this. This is devotion. Just this freedom from expectation, what should come to me? What will I get? If you just become free from that, your life is a blessed life.

**Source language:** English
