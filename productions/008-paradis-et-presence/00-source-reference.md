# Source reference — 008

> The greatest crime that's been done to humanity is the idea of heaven. Just this idea that there is a better place to live than this has caused such damage to the way people live here. It's been sold to you as an idea. It is a most disastrous idea and a criminal idea that there is a better place than this elsewhere. This is the place. If you do everything that you do with absolute involvement and willingness, this is your heaven. If you do it, everything that you do with resistance and lack of involvement, this will definitely feel like hell.

Editorial note: preserve the provocative idea while avoiding dogmatic attacks on religious belief or dismissing real suffering. Focus on presence and how we inhabit this life.
