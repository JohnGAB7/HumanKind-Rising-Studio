# Direction visuelle — 010

- **Format:** vertical 9:16, cinematic, premium, no recognizable faces.
- **Opening:** city bustle, rain on glass, phone notifications, tight abstract details; visual tension without portraying identifiable people.
- **Shift:** a hand releases a clenched grip; sunlight through a window; slow breath suggested by curtains or grass moving.
- **Contrast:** harsh, fragmented light gradually becomes warm and calm. Avoid simplistic “happy vs sad” stock imagery.
- **Flower metaphor:** macro flower opening, dew, petals moving gently; fragrance represented abstractly by light, wind, or drifting pollen.
- **Ending:** flower in soft light; quiet open space; signature HumanKind Rising.
- Palette: deep blue/turquoise transitioning to soft gold and white. Restrained typography.
- No speaker likeness, no generated voice, no music/audio.
