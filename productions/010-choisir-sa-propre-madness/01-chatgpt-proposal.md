# Editorial proposal — 010

## Title
**Ne laisse personne choisir ton état intérieur**

## Core idea
We cannot always control what happens around us, or prevent every emotion. But we can gradually stop making our inner state entirely dependent on others' actions. Joy is not a performance: it is a quality we can nurture, and it changes the atmosphere we bring into our relationships.

## Angle
From “you can't make me go mad” toward a grounded message of emotional freedom—not invulnerability, suppression, or blame.
