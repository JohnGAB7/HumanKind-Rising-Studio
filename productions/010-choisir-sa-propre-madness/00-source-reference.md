# Source reference — 010

## Original teaching (English)

> People ask me, they said, bro, will you ever get stressed? Will you not get angry? Will you not go crazy? I have not given the privilege to anybody. If I want to go mad, I will choose my own kind of madness. You can't make me go mad. You cannot. I'm not given the privilege to anybody. You must also do this. This is the best thing you can do to yourself and everybody around you. I'm asking you a simple question. If you are given a choice, would you like to live and work with joyful people or miserable people? Joyful people. I want you to please, please, please remember everybody else is expecting the same thing. If you're really concerned about people around you, the best thing you can do is that you are like a fragrant flower all the time

## Editorial focus

Emotional autonomy: not handing others the power to determine one's inner state. This is not denial of emotion or a demand to be cheerful constantly; it is an invitation to cultivate a way of being that brings less suffering and more ease to oneself and others.
