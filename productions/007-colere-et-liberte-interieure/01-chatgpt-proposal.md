# Production 007 — Editorial proposal

## Core idea
We cannot control everything others do or everything that happens around us. But we can learn to understand our reactions and gradually gain more freedom in how we respond.

## Editorial safeguards
- Do not shame people for experiencing anger.
- Distinguish an emotion arising from the choice to feed it or act from it.
- Avoid promising total control over emotions.
- Preserve boundaries and the ability to respond firmly to harmful situations.

## Proposed title
La colère : un choix que nous n'avons pas appris à faire

## Hook
Tu n'es pas obligé de te mettre en colère.

## Direction
An invitation to self-observation and understanding, not suppression.
